Both faces carry the family name "Source Han Sans SC" internally. They are embedded in
Leaf.exe and used through the paper typeface declared in src/Leaf/Views/Theme.xaml, so the
reading page renders in them on a machine that never installed the font. Nothing is
subsetted, renamed or otherwise modified, and the faces are never distributed on their own:
the licence travels inside the executable with them.
